Getting Started

A quick orientation for the Monte Carlo Portfolio Simulator.

1

Two tabs, two questions

The simulator has two main tabs:

  • Single Allocation — runs many simulations at one stock/bond split. How wide is the range of outcomes for this mix?
  • Frontier Sweep — runs simulations across every split and maps out the efficient frontier, the curve of best achievable return at every level of risk. How does the tradeoff shift across all allocations?
2

Single Allocation: the spread at one mix

This is what opens first. The Gain vs SD plot shows a cloud of outcomes — each dot is one simulation, with separate dots for the rebalanced and non-rebalanced strategies (the About page explains why both matter). The sub-tabs show the same simulations from different angles:

  • Final Densities — distribution of ending portfolio values
  • Trajectories — portfolio value paths over time
  • Cost Profiles — rebalancing transaction costs
  • Single Sim Explorer — unpack the machinery behind any individual run
3

Frontier Sweep: the big picture

Switch to the Frontier Sweep tab to see all allocations at once, preloaded with a 5,000-sim sweep. Two frontier curves (rebalanced vs. non-rebalanced) show how rebalancing shifts the tradeoff.

  • ← → arrows — step from 0% stocks to 100% and watch the highlighted cloud move along the curve
  • Scenario grid — click “Explore alternative correlations and rebalancing intervals” to compare across four correlation levels and three rebalancing intervals. Try negative correlation to see the frontier reshape.
4

Zoom and adjust the view

  • Zoom — drag a rectangle on any plot. On the Frontier Explorer, the zoom persists as you step through allocations.
  • Cloud Opacity — fade the simulation cloud to isolate point estimates, or increase it to study the full distribution.
5

Run your own

Adjust parameters in the sidebar and hit Run. Single Allocation runs one stock/bond split; Frontier Sweep sweeps all allocations. High sim counts take longer — the app will show a progress bar.

The spread is the story — point estimates summarize, but the cloud of outcomes shows you what's actually possible. For more on the methodology, see How It Works. For motivation and context, see About.
